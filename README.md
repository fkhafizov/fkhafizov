## About

My work combines mathematical methods with practical machine-learning systems.

Current interests include:

* graph distances, Cayley and Schreier graphs
* graph embeddings and geometric representations
* reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making
* natural-language processing and information retrieval
* scientific computing and mathematical visualization
* machine learning for telecommunications and network optimization

I previously worked as a Distinguished Fellow and data scientist at Verizon, developing machine-learning and optimization methods for wireless networks. I also teach applied deep learning at The University of Texas at Dallas.

## Selected Research Direction

A recurring theme in my research is the computation and approximation of graph distance,

$$
d_G(X,Y),
$$

and the construction of representations that preserve the geometry of large discrete state spaces.

These questions connect graph algorithms, representation learning, combinatorics, optimization, and mathematical physics.
